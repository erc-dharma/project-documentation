# SIDDHAM (encoded editions)

This [repository](https://github.com/erc-dharma/siddham) contains the editions endoced in EpiDoc in the course of the ERC project Beyond Boundaries, largely harmonised with the DHARMA encoding scheme.

The original files from Beyond Boundaries are available on [Zenodo](https://zenodo.org/records/2574901) along with the corresponding [metadata and bibliography](https://zenodo.org/records/2553133).
Notes on the conversion of the original encoding to the DHARMA scheme are available [here](https://github.com/erc-dharma/siddham/blob/3e68bdacae576304f3350baff0110bf6f7efab68/Siddham%20to%20DHARMA%20notes%202023.docx).
